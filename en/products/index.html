AppSheet Systems

AppSheet is a no-code app platform from Google. It lets you quickly build custom apps from Google Sheets, Excel or other data sources.

Features

  • Data connections and views: Connect Google Sheets or Excel and get forms, charts, dashboards and other mobile-friendly screens.
  • Offline use and sync: Enter data offline; it syncs automatically once you're back online.
  • User management: Basic permission control, so you can share it with the people you work with.
  • Works across devices: Computer browsers, Android and iOS phones, tablets and more.
