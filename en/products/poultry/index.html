Broiler Farm Record System

The Broiler Farm Record System is a record-keeping tool designed for small and mid-sized broiler farmers. Your data stays in your own hands and isn't tied to any equipment brand.

Daily records

Water use, deaths and culls, drinking water and medication. A few minutes a day is enough.

Batch settlement

Once a batch is cleared, feed conversion ratio (FCR) and livability are calculated automatically. No calculator needed.

Analysis charts

Every batch's numbers become charts, so you can compare batches and spot what to adjust for the next one.

Your data stays yours

Not tied to any climate-control brand. Switch equipment or systems without losing your data.

Aimed at contract broiler farmers in central and southern Taiwan (water-curtain houses, mainstream breeds like Arbor Acres and Ross), not a general livestock tool. It comes from first-hand work experience, and it's built to fit real needs: not only recording, but seeing how each batch changes in the charts, finding what can be improved or adjusted, and raising livability.

Status: In development. Not yet open for trial or subscription. If you'd like to hear as soon as it launches, please write to me.
