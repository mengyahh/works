# There's always an understory.

I'm Meng Ya, a freelancer of five years with a slightly unconventional career. If you only look at the final output, it's often hard to tell what my expertise actually is — it can look like I just do odd jobs.
What I offer is part consultant, part companion. I'm not a content producer who just does what they're told: I get to know you, find the real pain points, and then decide what to make and how — a full service from analysis and planning to making.

- **Custom AppSheet management systems**：Made to order, simple or complex. A system helps you organise things and see where you stand, and the data you build up stays in your own hands.
- **Event planning**：Planning and running talks, small markets and workshops, with social media graphics and printed materials.
- **Issue outreach**：Posters, handbooks, themed websites and videos that uncover and spread stories.
